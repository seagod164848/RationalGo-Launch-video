# "Before Sunrise" — RationalGo launch film

**Length:** ~75 seconds · **Format:** 16:9 master, 9:16 cut-downs
**Principle:** This is a film about *her*, not about the software. R.go is never the hero; it is the quiet thing working in the background of her day. Product appears only as glances: a laptop on her knees, a phone buzzing on a café table, a notification she half-reads while living her life.

---

## The character

**Maya Reyes, 29.** Lives in a fourth-floor walk-up in Washington Heights. Works mornings at a coffee cart in Midtown. Has sketchbooks full of clothes she's never made. Not a "founder" yet, just someone who notices things.

**Her brand (born in this film):** **ALBA**, clothes for women who start before sunrise. (*Alba* = dawn.)

**Look:** 35mm, warm tungsten against cool blue city light, handheld, shallow depth of field. Palette moves from pre-dawn blue → Times Square neon → golden hour → the next dawn.

## Screen-flow shot list (capture from the real product)

Record at 2560×1440 in a clean browser profile with no bookmarks bar, and use a cursor highlight. Each capture is only on screen for 2–4 s, so they should be **fast and legible**, not tutorials.

1. **Prompt:** rationalgo.ai home → type *"Build my clothing brand. It's called ALBA, clothes for women who start before sunrise."* → Enter.
2. **Website:** the ALBA site generating: hero, product grid, checkout.
3. **Suppliers:** a list of 10 suppliers (Guangzhou, Dongguan, Hangzhou…) → *"Emails sent"* state → the follow-up thread view.
4. **Brand book:** logo, palette, typography, and tone-of-voice pages.
5. **Social:** content calendar → scheduled posts → published confirmation for Instagram + LinkedIn.

Mock versions of these screens (in `screens/`) keep the edit moving until the real captures are dropped in. They use the same timing and framing, so a real capture can replace a mock 1:1.
